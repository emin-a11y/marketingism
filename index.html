Good to know

Baby shower FAQs

Have a question that's not here? Give us a call.

Can we bring our own food and cake?
Yes. You provide the food and drinks from any vendor you like. The venues are set up for drop-off catering (no stove or oven on site).
How many guests can we invite?
The Petite Venue hosts up to 30 guests. The Studio Venue and Sherman Oaks each host up to 50.
Is there parking for guests?
Burbank venues have reserved lot spaces plus unrestricted street parking. Sherman Oaks has a large rear lot.
Can we play music?
Every venue has a built-in sound system you can connect to over Wi-Fi.
